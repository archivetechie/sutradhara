# Code of conduct

We expect contributors and maintainers to communicate with care, technical
honesty, and respect. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sustained
personal attacks, doxxing, and publication of private operational data are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Disagreement about design and evidence is welcome. Critique the change, state
the risk concretely, and give others room to correct mistakes. Maintainers may
edit or remove abusive material and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community or archive operations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainers or the
private vulnerability-reporting channel. Do not publish sensitive details in an
issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical,
without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
